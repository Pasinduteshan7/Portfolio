01 / Background

Engineering Mindset & Narrative

Bridging low-level systems and high-level artificial intelligence with rigorous engineering principles.

I am a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Ruhuna. Rather than limiting myself to surface-level application building, I focus on understanding systems deeply: from implementing cryptographic Proof-of-Work blockchains in raw Go to writing custom CT medical image slice extraction algorithms in Python.

My engineering work covers three primary pillars:

  • Machine Learning & MLOps: Training PyTorch and scikit-learn models with reproducible tracking in MLflow & DVC, deploying inference microservices, and utilizing local LLMs.
  • Polyglot & Enterprise Full-Stack: Architecting multi-backend solutions across Spring Boot (Java), Go, and Node.js with Next.js 15, PostgreSQL, and WebSockets.
  • DevOps & Cloud Automation: Automating builds with Jenkins CI/CD, containerizing via Docker, and provisioning infrastructure using Terraform on AWS.

Outside of software, I actively give back to the student community through Mehewara by conducting educational seminars for high school students, and hone leadership and grit through trail expeditions with the Gaveshakayo Hiking Club.
